About Me

Hi, I’m Shilpa, a Program Manager with experience at Amazon and an AI Researcher focused on cybersecurity. I hold a master’s degree in Artificial Intelligence (cum laude) and have worked on a range of impactful projects that blend engineering, machine learning, and innovation.

At Amazon, I led programs that improved internal processes and customer experience. As an AI Researcher, I contributed to a cybersecurity project where I developed attack simulation scripts and helped train machine learning models for threat detection. I also served as a Teaching Assistant for MATLAB and Python, a role I took very seriously. Despite commuting over 40 miles round trip between campuses by bus, I made it a point to never miss a single class, ensuring I could support students consistently and effectively.

My dedication to academic and research excellence was recognized through the prestigious Pioneer Competitive Research Award, an honor awarded to only the top-performing students. During my graduate studies, I built several AI-driven systems including a real-time object detection model using YOLO on Raspberry Pi4, a deep learning-powered chat application, and an autonomous robot for smart inventory management.

Beyond technical work, I actively contributed to the campus community. As President of the MS Business Analytics Club, I organized AI-focused workshops and events. As Vice President of the Melody Mosaic Asian Music Club, I helped promote cultural expression through music and performance.

Outside of work and academics, I’m passionate about outdoor sports like badminton, table tennis, and staying active. I believe in giving my full effort to every opportunity — whether it’s teaching, leading, or learning — and I’m always open to meaningful conversations and collaborations. Feel free to reach out if you’d like to connect!
