About Me

My journey into Artificial Intelligence began during my bachelor’s degree, where I was honored with the National Technical Symposium Award. At that time, I was learning programming languages like C++ and Python, though I initially understood them mostly through gaming and futuristic ideas—like robots cleaning houses—that were just beginning to take shape. Working on an industrial-level project during my undergraduate studies was a turning point. It made me realize the critical importance of precision and cemented my passion for AI. When I began my master’s in Artificial Intelligence, I shifted from theory to building real-time projects designed to enhance people’s lives and simplify everyday tasks. I developed chatbots for my college to provide students instant information about courses and parking availability. I also created a compact, real-time object detection system aimed at preventing traffic accidents. I designed strategies to tackle complex problems efficiently, emphasizing the importance of algorithms in AI solutions. One standout project involved helping autonomous robots navigate a storage room to pick and place items, analyzing task efficiency based on operator gender and time taken. This experience deepened my understanding of human-robot interaction and optimization. I expanded my skills by analyzing diverse data types using data science and machine learning, selecting the best models to build accurate prediction websites. Most recently, I contributed to a cybersecurity project that prevents cyberattacks by creating transparent attack-cycle trees and clear, machine-learning-friendly scripts to improve pattern recognition. Alongside AI and cybersecurity, I gained hands-on experience developing websites using AWS storage, integrating front-end and back-end technologies to deliver user-friendly platforms. Throughout my journey, I have grown from a curious beginner to a passionate AI professional. I am dedicated, detail-oriented, and a strong collaborator who believes in continuous learning and using technology responsibly to create meaningful impact. I look forward to connecting and collaborating on projects that push technological boundaries and improve lives.
