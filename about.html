About Me

About Me

My journey into Artificial Intelligence began during my bachelor’s degree, where I was honored with the National Technical Symposium Award. Back then, I was learning programming languages like C++ and Python, though I initially only used them for gaming and exploring futuristic ideas like robots cleaning houses.

An industrial-level project during my undergraduate studies became a turning point. It taught me the importance of precision and solidified my passion for AI and engineering.

When I started my master’s in Artificial Intelligence, I moved beyond theory to build real-time projects aimed at improving everyday life. I developed a chatbot for my college to help students access course and parking information quickly. I also built a compact, real-time object detection system designed to prevent traffic accidents.

I worked on strategies to solve complex problems effectively, emphasizing the critical role of algorithms. One notable project involved programming autonomous robots to navigate a storage room, picking and placing items while analyzing task efficiency based on operator gender and time. This deepened my understanding of human-robot interaction and optimization.

I gained expertise in analyzing diverse data sets using data science and machine learning, selecting the best models to create accurate prediction websites. Most recently, I contributed to a cybersecurity project focused on preventing cyberattacks by developing clear, machine-learning-ready scripts and transparent attack-cycle trees to improve threat detection.

Alongside AI and cybersecurity, I have practical experience building websites using AWS storage, combining front-end and back-end technologies to create user-friendly platforms.
